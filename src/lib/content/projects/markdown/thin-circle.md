Thin Circle is a twenty-minute, single-channel video work exploring mediated nature. Filmed from a fixed point looking out over open ocean, it follows a rainstorm as it moves in across the water: the horizon softening, dissolving into grey, and slowly returning.

The work carries the experience of an oceanic rain storm into the form of slow media. Without cuts, narrative or spectacle, it asks us to sit with the sublime elements that we so often overlook, and to notice how our encounters with nature are increasingly framed, recorded and replayed through screens.
